### **Privacy and Number Separation**

Some users prefer not to share their personal phone number with every online service.

A virtual number may provide separation, but it should not be treated as a guarantee of anonymity. Providers may retain transaction information, and numbers may be reassigned.

### **Temporary Access**

A temporary number can be useful when a legitimate service allows one-time verification and does not require ongoing access to the same number.

It is not a good choice for accounts that may later require password recovery or additional security checks.

### **Regional Testing**

Businesses and developers may need to test how their own applications handle phone numbers from different regions.

For this purpose, authorized test numbers and dedicated SMS infrastructure are preferable when available.

**Important:** Always follow the terms of the website or application being accessed. Do not use temporary numbers to bypass security controls, impersonate other users, or create accounts in violation of platform rules.
